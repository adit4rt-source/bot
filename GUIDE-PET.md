## 1. Konsep Inti

Pet punya **2 peran**:
- **Bonus pasif** (otomatis): nambah income/XP/luck kamu selama pet aktif, kenyang & senang.
- **Battle & aktivitas**: Hunt, Dungeon, Boss, Expedition, PvP, World Boss.

Kamu bisa punya banyak pet, tapi **cuma 1 yang aktif** dalam satu waktu. Ganti via **🔄 Swap**.

## 4. Stats, Class & Element

Saat didapat, pet di-generate stats acak sesuai tier: **HP, ATK, DEF, SPD, CRIT**.

Contoh range (makin tinggi tier makin besar):
- Common: HP 60–100, ATK 10–25
- Mythic: HP 200–300, ATK 50–85
- GOD: HP 400–550, ATK 100–150

- **🏷️ Class** (warrior/tank/mage/ranger/healer): **label kosmetik** — TIDAK memengaruhi battle. Battle pakai stats mentah + element + skill.
- **🔥 Element** (fire/water/nature/electric/dark/light): **berpengaruh besar di battle** (lihat [Element Matchup](#9-element-matchup)).

## 8. Skill Battle

Pet unlock **1 skill acak per tier** otomatis saat naik level — maksimal **5 skill**:

| Tier | Unlock Lv | Pilihan skill (acak 1) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **10** | Power Strike (2x dmg) · Shield Wall (-50% dmg) · Quick Heal (+20% HP) |
| 2 | **30** | Critical Surge (+30% crit) · Elemental Blast (2.5x, bonus elemen) · Life Drain (1.5x + heal 50%) |
| 3 | **60** | Berserk (+50% ATK / -30% DEF) · Iron Fortress (-80% dmg) · **Resurrection (bangkit 30% HP, 1x/battle)** |
| 4 | **100** | Ultimate Strike (**4x dmg**) · Divine Shield (kebal 1 turn) · Omega Heal (+50% HP) |
| **5 Ascendant** | **150** | Elemental Catastrophe · Time Stop · Blood Pact · Aegis of Gods · Soul Link · Omega Burst · Void Rend · Genesis Light |

Skill dipakai otomatis (~20% peluang/turn jika tidak sedang cooldown).

**Skill Reroll:** Beli **📖 Skill Tome** di Nightmare Shop → `/pet` → ⬆️ More → **Skill Reroll** → pilih tier. 1 tome = 1 reroll (skill diganti acak di tier yang sama).

**Skill Milestone (bonus PASIF tambahan):** Lv.20 +2% Money · Lv.50 +2% XP · Lv.100 +3% Fish Luck · Lv.200 +5% Farm.

## 10. Aktivitas Pet

### 🏹 Hunt
Kirim pet berburu **10–20 menit**. 80% sukses → **100–400 money** (scaling level) + EXP + 25% chance material. Buff pasif MATI selama hunt.

### 🏰 Dungeon — *aktif, cepat, COUNTER elemen*
Instan (animasi 3 detik), CD beberapa menit. **COUNTER** elemen musuh untuk +25% dmg. Sumber utama **relic & material gear**. **Ada risiko**: kalah = kehilangan money kecil (dibatasi cap per tier, bukan % total saldo). Reward & loot naik per tier.

### 👹 Boss
- **Solo** (CD 10 menit): money besar + refine stone + relic. Boss endgame (Void Emperor, Ancient God) bisa drop **Mythic Fragment / Awakening Crystal**.
- **Party Raid** (1–10 player): damage tiap member di-scale elemen vs boss.

### 🌊 Expedition — *pasif AFK, SYNERGY elemen*
Kirim pet **2–8 jam** (pet terkunci). Tanpa risiko. **Bawa pet se-elemen dengan tema zona** → **Synergy: +25% money, +20% EXP, +15% drop**. Fokus **EXP massal + booster/consumable**.

Tema zona: 🌲 Hutan = nature · 🏔️ Gua = electric · 🏛️ Reruntuhan = light · 🌊 Laut = water · 🌑 Shadow = dark · 🗼 Menara = fire

> 🎯 **Beda kunci:** Dungeon = *lawan* elemen musuh (counter aktif). Expedition = *samakan* elemen zona (synergy pasif).

## 11. Evolution

Pet tertentu berevolusi otomatis di level tertentu (20 / 50 / 100 / 150) jadi pet lebih kuat — stats di-regenerate sesuai tier baru.
Contoh: Fire Fox → Phoenix (Lv.100) → Divine Phoenix (Lv.150).

## 12. Fusion

Gabung **2 pet tier sama** (harus non-aktif) → 1 pet **tier lebih tinggi**:

| Tier | Biaya | Sukses | Hasil |
|---|---|---|---|
| Common | 3.000 | 90% | Uncommon |
| Uncommon | 10.000 | 75% | Rare |
| Rare | 30.000 | 60% | Epic |
| Epic | 80.000 | 45% | Legendary |
| Legendary | 200.000 | 30% | Mythic |

- **Sukses**: kedua pet hilang, lahir pet baru tier atas (stats base + 10% gabungan parent, mewarisi class/element parent terkuat).
- **Gagal**: kehilangan **1 pet acak** dari pasangan (biaya tetap kepotong). ⚠️

## 13. Relic & Refine

Relic (Weapon / Armor / Accessory) drop dari Dungeon/Boss, kasih +ATK/DEF/SPD/CRIT. **Refine** pakai 🪨 Refine Stone untuk upgrade (+1). Setelah +10 rate turun; 🛡️ Protection Stone mencegah turun level saat gagal.

## 14. Abilities (Pasif di Dunia)

Pet Lv.30+ buka slot ability (swap cooldown 24 jam). Aktif otomatis selama pet sehat:

- **Tier 1 / Slot 1 (Lv.30):** Auto-Fish · Auto-Water · Passive Income (100 + lvl×25 /30mnt) · Passive XP · Slow Hunger
- **Tier 2 / Slot 2 (Lv.50):** +15% jual market · /daily +30% · Streak Guardian · Quest Ace · -15% harga shop
- **Tier 3 / Slot 3 (Lv.100):** Double Catch · Green Thumb · Relic Hunter (+15% relic) · Crit Master (+10% crit) · Swift Explorer (-25% expedition)
- **Tier 4 Ascendant / Slot 4 (Lv.150 + Awakening ★2+):**
  - **Boss Scavenger** — +10% qty loot boss
  - **Arena Veteran** — +5% AP arena, −10% CD fight
  - **Egg Whisperer** — +2% tilt ke tier lebih tinggi saat hatch egg
  - **Relic Polish** — +8% success rate refine
  - **Nightmare Runner** — +1 entry Nightmare Dungeon / hari

## 🗺️ Roadmap Player Baru

1. **Beli pet** + Feed/Play biar bonus pasif aktif.
2. **Hunt** rutin buat naik level (unlock skill Lv.10).
3. Buka **Dungeon** → relic & material; bawa pet yang **counter** elemen musuh.
4. **Expedition** AFK → EXP + booster; bawa pet **se-elemen** zona (synergy).
5. Kumpulkan duit → **Egg** buat tier tinggi, atau **Fusion** naik tier.
6. Push ke **Lv.200** → **Awakening** buat endgame.
7. **Lv.150** → Skill Tier 5 Ascendant; ★2 → Ability Slot 4.
8. **Nightmare** harian → Token → Skill Tome / material endgame.
